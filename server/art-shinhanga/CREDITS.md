# Print credits

Shin-hanga ("new prints", early-modern Japanese art from the 1900s–1930s) by Hiroshi Yoshida (d. 1950), Ohara Koson (d. 1945), Takahashi Shōtei (d. 1945) and Hashiguchi Goyō (d. 1921). All are public domain in the UK and Japan. They were downloaded from **Wikimedia Commons**, and many of the scans come from the Rijksmuseum Amsterdam (CC0) and the Wereldmuseum. `manifest.json` lists each file's Commons page and licence.

Kawase Hasui was left out on purpose: his work stays under copyright in the UK until 2028.
